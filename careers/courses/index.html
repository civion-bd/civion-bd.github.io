Course 01

insights ETABS — Applied Structural Analysis

Model setup and verification, load combinations per BNBC 2020 / ASCE 7-22, seismic and gravity design, and foundation interaction — taught so a student understands why a model behaves a certain way, not just how to build one.

ETABSBNBC 2020ASCE 7-22
  • ETABS model setup — geometry, sections, materials, and load cases
  • Load combinations per BNBC 2020 and ASCE 7-22, including live/dead/wind/seismic
  • Response spectrum and equivalent static seismic analysis
  • Gravity and lateral member design checks (RCC and steel)
  • Foundation interaction and reaction transfer
  • Model verification — sanity checks, warning diagnosis, and results QA
